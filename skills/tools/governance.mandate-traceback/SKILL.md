# Mandate Traceback

## Purpose
Trace an action back to procedural, legal, or political authority claims.

## When To Use
Use when a user needs mandate basis or authority lineage.

## When Not To Use
Do not use to generate political scenarios.

## Inputs
Action, mandate references, case graph, and receipts.

## Outputs
Authority chain, gaps, and citation notes.

## Context Requirements
Needs governance ontology and source-backed claims.

## Composition
Can chain into Brief Exporter and Commitment Check.

## Examples
Trace a monitoring action to mandate authority.

## Pitfalls
Do not overstate legal authority from political language.
