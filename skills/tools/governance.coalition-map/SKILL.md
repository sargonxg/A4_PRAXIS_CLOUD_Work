# Coalition Map

## Purpose
Map supportive, blocking, and swing actors around a decision.

## When To Use
Use for mandate, vote, or coalition planning.

## When Not To Use
Do not use for deep narrative propagation analysis.

## Inputs
Policy action, actors, positions, and source receipts.

## Outputs
Coalition groups, swing factors, and engagement notes.

## Context Requirements
Needs user role, governance case graph, and actor coverage.

## Composition
Can chain into Veto Probability and Brief Exporter.

## Examples
Map coalition support before a mandate renewal.

## Pitfalls
Separate public positions from inferred incentives.
