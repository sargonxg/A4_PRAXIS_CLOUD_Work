# Position vs Interest

## Purpose
Separate stated positions from underlying interests.

## When To Use
Use for mediation or negotiation preparation.

## When Not To Use
Do not use for legal mandate traceback or narrative spread.

## Inputs
Party statements, case graph, prompt, and optional focused party.

## Outputs
Position/interest table, confidence notes, and citation gaps.

## Context Requirements
Needs mediator role/context, case actors/claims/interests, and optional methodology docs.

## Composition
Can chain into ZOPA Finder and Mediation Brief.

## Examples
Separate a party demand from its recognition and security interests.

## Pitfalls
Label inferred interests clearly.
