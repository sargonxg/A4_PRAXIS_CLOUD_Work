# Fact-Check Traceback

## Purpose
Trace a claim to receipts, conflicts, uncertainty, and open gaps.

## When To Use
Use whenever a factual claim needs provenance.

## When Not To Use
Do not use to predict future scenarios.

## Inputs
Claim text, graph claim nodes, source receipts, and prompt.

## Outputs
Traceback, confidence, contradictions, and citations.

## Context Requirements
Needs source and claim context; works across ontologies.

## Composition
Can feed Contradiction Finder and Brief Exporter.

## Examples
Trace a casualty or commitment claim to receipts.

## Pitfalls
Do not resolve contradictions without source hierarchy.
