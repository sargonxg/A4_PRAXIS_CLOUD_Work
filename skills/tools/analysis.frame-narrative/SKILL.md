## Purpose
`analysis.frame-narrative` surfaces how claims are framed and who carries each narrative.

## When To Use
Use for communication risk, contested legitimacy, public positioning, and narrative conflict analysis.

## When Not To Use
Do not use as a fact-check substitute; pair with source review and contradiction checks.

## Inputs
Requires `caseId`; optional date range, actor filter, audience, or narrative question.

## Outputs
Returns frame clusters, actor-frame mapping, audience exposure, shift timeline, citations, and gaps.

## Context Requirements
Needs claims, source excerpts, actors, channels, or audience notes from the active Context.

## Composition
Can feed Contradiction Finder, Deep Research, Talking Points, and Memo Drafter.

## Examples
Foreign policy: sovereignty frame analysis. Domestic policy: regulatory burden versus safety frames. Corporate: transparency versus greenwashing frames.

## Pitfalls
Do not overstate audience effects without evidence; separate frame detection from impact claims.
