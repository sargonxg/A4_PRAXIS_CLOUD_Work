# ZOPA Finder

## Purpose
Estimate zones of possible agreement from interests, constraints, and guarantees.

## When To Use
Use after positions and interests are at least partially mapped.

## When Not To Use
Do not use as a first ingestion or fact-checking step.

## Inputs
Party interests, red lines, guarantees, graph snippets, and prompt.

## Outputs
ZOPA candidates, risks, and confidence bands.

## Context Requirements
Needs mediation ontology context and source-backed interests.

## Composition
Can chain from Position vs Interest and into Mediation Brief.

## Examples
Find revenue-sharing ranges that might satisfy both sides.

## Pitfalls
Do not make agreement feasibility sound deterministic.
