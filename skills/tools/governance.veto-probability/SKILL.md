# Veto Probability

## Purpose
Score likely veto points around mandates, coalitions, or policy actions.

## When To Use
Use for governance and diplomatic decision planning.

## When Not To Use
Do not use for source extraction or generic risk scoring.

## Inputs
Mandate question, actor map, voting context, and source receipts.

## Outputs
Veto drivers, probability bands, and evidence caveats.

## Context Requirements
Needs governance ontology context, user role, and coalition actor coverage.

## Composition
Can chain into Coalition Map and Brief Exporter.

## Examples
Assess veto risk for a mandate renewal paragraph.

## Pitfalls
Use bands, not false precision.
