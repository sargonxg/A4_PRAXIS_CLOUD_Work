# Key Assumptions Check

## Purpose
Surface stated and unstated assumptions underlying an analysis. Heuer-Pherson Structured Analytic Techniques attribution is required in user-visible outputs.

## When To Use
Use before a memo, briefing, or policy recommendation is finalized, especially when conclusions depend on uncertain actor intent or fragile evidence.

## When Not To Use
Do not use as a replacement for source discovery or as a truth validator for unsupported claims.

## Inputs
Memo text, analysis text, case graph claims, source receipts, user confidence tolerance, and optional focused memo block.

## Outputs
Assumption inventory, support-level classification, user verdict table, evidence gaps, and memo annex content.

## Context Requirements
Benefits from graph claims and source receipts. Runs gracefully on raw text and labels unsupported assumptions clearly.

## Composition
Can append a KAC annex to Memo Studio and can chain into Premortem or Red Team Analysis for additional challenge.

## Examples
Check a mediation brief for hidden assumptions about party willingness, mandate durability, or humanitarian access.

## Pitfalls
Do not convert an assumption into a fact. Keep unsupported assumptions visible until the user accepts or rejects them.
