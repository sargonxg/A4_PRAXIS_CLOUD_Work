# Frame Cascade

## Purpose
Track how a narrative frame moves across claims, messengers, audiences, and events.

## When To Use
Use for narrative or information-environment analysis.

## When Not To Use
Do not use for mediation package design.

## Inputs
Frame, claim nodes, messengers, sources, and prompt.

## Outputs
Frame path, amplification notes, and evidence gaps.

## Context Requirements
Needs narrative ontology context and source provenance.

## Composition
Can chain into Counter-Narrative Finder and Brief Exporter.

## Examples
Trace a sovereignty frame across public statements.

## Pitfalls
Do not infer coordination without evidence.
