# Brief Exporter

## Purpose
Export selected outputs into a decision brief with trust metadata.

## When To Use
Use when analysis needs a portable brief or memo-ready artifact.

## When Not To Use
Do not use as the first analytical step on an empty case.

## Inputs
Tool outputs, memo template, context snapshot, and citation map.

## Outputs
Brief sections, source notes, and export-ready blocks.

## Context Requirements
Needs user output preferences and source-backed case context.

## Composition
Can consume most tool outputs and feed Memo Drafter.

## Examples
Export contradiction and coalition findings into a policy brief.

## Pitfalls
Do not drop uncertainty or citation gaps.
