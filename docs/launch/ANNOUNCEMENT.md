# PRAXIS Launch Announcement Draft

Date: 2026-05-06

PRAXIS by TACITUS is a policy and conflict-intelligence workbench for turning sources, case context, graph structure, and analyst judgment into decision-ready work products.

This launch foundation includes:

- A unified case Workbench.
- Context health and symbolic firewall trust metadata.
- Ontology-backed graph structure over the ACO primitive substrate.
- A canonical graph view.
- A curated 16-tool marketplace.
- A Memo Studio foundation for mandate briefs, mediation summaries, policy memos, sit-reps, frame analysis, and talking points.

Known v1.1 priorities are memo persistence/export, Postgres KG repositories, full marketplace execution, admin consolidation, and operational diagnostics.
