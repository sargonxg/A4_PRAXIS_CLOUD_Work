# Phase 08 - operate a text-first paid desk

Additional required reading: [research/SOLO_FOUNDER_EXECUTION.md](../../research/SOLO_FOUNDER_EXECUTION.md), [research/MULTILINGUAL_EVALUATION.md](../../research/MULTILINGUAL_EVALUATION.md). Use these as design inputs; verify current provider/repository contracts in this checkout.

Create the customer desk setup workflow: mandate, audience, languages, source pack, question, review cadence, permitted collectors, quotas and owner/reviewer access. Make source-pack coverage and country-context maturity visible. A selectable country without a curated pack remains useful for supplied material and discovery, with its missing coverage declared.

Implement panel-versioned metrics for incoming source families, topic/frame activity, collector completeness within configured windows, analysis/review backlog and artifact usage. Declare denominators, bins, exclusions and source-panel changes. Exclude syndicated copies from independent-evidence counts. Volume anomaly screening may use a robust baseline, minimum sample size and data-health gate; it is an alert candidate, not proof of influence or danger.

Implement versioned evaluation fixtures and an analyst labeling lane. The initial gold set target is 200 records across at least three countries and two languages. Include ambiguity, negation, mixed scripts, quotation, aliases, temporal scopes, missing dates and misleading images where supported. Keep development fixtures separate from held-out evaluation. Measure model-plus-reviewed-capsule performance against raw-model and keyword baselines, including whether context adds errors.

Expose tenant/desk usage, actual tokens, object storage, provider costs, collector errors, review effort and budget-stop reasons. The blueprint's illustrative cost/pricing assumptions are not account invoices or validated buyer willingness to pay. Add a runbook for failed collectors, stale source panels, source takedown and model drift.

Acceptance: a desk can be configured, operated, paused and exported without developer database edits; failures and costs remain visible; changing a source panel cannot silently contaminate a trend baseline; evaluation results include error examples and sample sizes. Proposed targets include zero accepted fabricated quotations and at least 95% precision for extracted evidence spans on the labeled set. Report measured results; do not invent labels or mark a missing benchmark passed. No outreach or customer messaging is required by this coding phase.
