# Solo founder execution and purchase test

This is a proposed operating plan. Timelines, capacity and prices are hypotheses rather than booked customers or measured unit economics.

## One commercially useful desk first

Keep global architecture but sell a narrow continuing question to a buyer with recurring briefings: a geopolitical advisory team, NGO security/policy unit, risk team or research organization. Prefer a desk for which the founder or a contracted analyst can review the required source languages. A ministry-wide platform sale is a longer procurement path; it should not be required to validate the first product.

The first purchase is a reviewed weekly brief plus an assessment-change note and a traceable dossier, supported by the portal. Buyers should pay for saved briefing time, better-supported judgments and continuity between updates. They are unlikely to value an extra graph view without a decision workflow.

## Capacity and cost controls

Set a hard maximum of concurrent watched desks and a per-desk daily collection/inference allowance. Text triage is broad; exact capture and structured analysis are selective; image/audio/video escalation requires a relevant question and an explicit allowance. Choose actual numbers only after a one-week measurement of sources, duplicate rate, acquisition success, model spend, review minutes and useful findings.

Make budget reservations transactional in the existing worker plane. Count failed attempts and transcription/storage as well as successful completions. Stop escalation when the budget is spent and show the deferred queue. License costs and analyst labor can dominate inference; do not infer commercial viability from a small token calculation.

## Sequence

| Gate | Concrete output | Evidence to proceed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline and integration | Existing PRAXIS route, scoped data contracts and permissioned capture | Phase 00-03 checks pass; tenant boundaries hold |
| Text desk | Attributed claims, multilingual evidence, review and reopen | Phase 04-05 journey; held-out evaluation and failure cases |
| Continuing deliverable | Pinned brief, material dissent and correction delta | Phase 06; analyst and buyer can inspect every key judgment |
| Operable watch | Durable jobs, budgets, coverage health and operator queue | Phase 07-08 plus one measured permissioned live desk |
| Paid pilot | A real recurring briefing question and agreed deliverables | Buyer payment, repeated use and measured review effort |
| Targeted media | Question-specific image/audio/video analysis | Entitled sources, language/media evaluation and budget gate |

Timebox research/build iterations, but do not pretend a week estimate is a release guarantee. Existing PRAXIS service maturity, access and the first buyer's language needs determine how much work remains.

## Customer conversations

Use the same recorded evaluation questions across potential buyers: What decision or briefing recurs? Which sources are currently tracked? Which claim or changing interpretation is hardest to substantiate? Who reviews it? What output is usable in their existing workflow? What procurement/security constraints apply? What would make a paid pilot successful?

Offer one concrete example packet: a desk overview, a two-page reviewed brief, its evidence receipts and a subsequent correction delta. Label synthetic examples. Ask the buyer to compare it with their current process, not to endorse a broad idea about AI intelligence.

## Success and stopping criteria

Before the pilot, agree on buyer usefulness, time saved, source traceability, reviewed error rate, turnaround and weekly founder effort. Track whether the buyer reuses the packet and returns for the next update. Expand only if delivery fits the founder's measured capacity and repeat revenue covers review, licensed sources and infrastructure. If the customer values the report but never uses the portal, sell the report first and use the portal as the analyst workbench.

Do not build a second application, buy a graph cluster, train a foundation model or add many MCPs before the current PRAXIS services and the text pilot are tested. The build kit already contains the work order. Phase 00 decides which existing components to reuse, and phase 08 documents the smallest sustainable operating setup.
