PRAXIS sells governed AI workspaces for serious policy work. Individual
analysts, mediation teams, research desks, public-interest organizations, and
institutions use PRAXIS to turn scattered context into sourceable memos,
briefs, handovers, and agent runs.

Revenue comes from monthly workspace subscriptions:

- Free: starter ContextCapsule workspace for evaluation.
- Practitioner: `$49/month` for individual analysts, policy officers, and
  researchers.
- Team: `$199/month per seat` for mediation teams, policy units, and research
  desks.
- Institution: custom pricing for organizations that need SSO, audit controls,
  data residency, private GCP deployment support, procurement support, and
  dedicated account coverage.

The product captures value by saving analysts time, preserving institutional
memory, improving source discipline, and making AI outputs reviewable enough
for policy, diplomacy, legal, humanitarian, public-sector, and strategic work.

## What PRAXIS Does

PRAXIS is a ContextCapsule-native policy workspace. A ContextCapsule packages
the working context behind a serious file: who is asking, what situation is in
scope, which sources are trusted, what timeline and stakeholders matter, what
rules apply, what output is needed, and what an AI agent is allowed to inherit.

The core loop is:

```text
Build ContextCapsule
  -> Ask PRAXIS
  -> Attach sources and documents
  -> Run capsule-aware tools or agents
  -> Review evidence, gaps, and approvals
  -> Draft memo, brief, talking points, or handover
```

## Problem

Policy teams do not mainly fail because they lack a chatbot. They fail because
the context needed for good judgment is scattered across documents, emails,
meetings, old memos, dashboards, source links, and people's heads.

Generic AI tools start from a blank prompt and often lose the provenance,
timeline, institutional constraints, and dissenting evidence that serious work
requires. When an analyst leaves, a director joins, a crisis changes, or an AI
agent takes over, the team often starts from zero again.

## Solution

PRAXIS makes context a managed object. It gives policy workers a simple Ask
PRAXIS surface, but the answer is scoped by reusable ContextCapsules, source
receipts, document digests, graph posture, tool routing, review gates, and
agent-run receipts.

The result is not just chat. It is a governed workflow from question to
evidence to memo to handover, with the context preserved for the next person or
agent.
